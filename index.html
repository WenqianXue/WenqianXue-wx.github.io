Learning · Control · Autonomy

Wenqian Xue

Postdoctoral Associate & Adjunct Professor
Nonlinear Controls and Robotics Laboratory
University of Florida

My research focuses on advancing safe, reliable, and interpretable learning and control for autonomous aerial, robotic, and multi-agent systems. I pursue this goal through two complementary directions: AI for Control, which develops learning-based methods for adaptive and safe control, objective and intent inference, and autonomous decision making; and Control for AI, which develops control-theoretic foundations for reliable generative and sequential learning, including safe trajectory and distribution generation. Specifically, my research lies at the intersection of control theory, (inverse) reinforcement learning, deep learning, game theory, and generative modeling, focusing on UAV and robotic applications.

Currently, I am a Postdoctoral Associate at the Nonlinear Control and Robotics (NCR) Laboratory in the Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, University of Florida. I am also an Adjunct Professor in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, and I teach Linear Control Systems. Before joining the NCR Laboratory in 2024, I was a Postdoctoral Associate at the Computational NeuroEngineering Laboratory in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, University of Florida. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering in 2022 and my M.S.E. in Control Engineering in 2018 from Northeastern University, China.
